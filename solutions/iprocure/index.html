Problem statement

Procurement breaks when work is spread across too many places.

Organizations often run procurement through disconnected systems, emails, spreadsheets, and manual approvals. That makes the full lifecycle hard to see, control, and improve.

Manual processing slows execution.

Purchase requests, purchase orders, invoices, and approvals take longer when teams rely on handoffs and repeated administration.

Vendor and delivery visibility stays limited.

Teams struggle to compare quotations, track partial deliveries, monitor pending quantities, and follow service milestones.

Finance control becomes harder to maintain.

Invoice mismatches, duplicate invoices, outstanding payments, and unclear spend data make risks and savings opportunities harder to identify.

Solution overview

One platform for the procurement lifecycle.

IProcure is designed as a centralized procurement management system that standardizes the journey from business requirement and purchase requisition to vendor sourcing, purchase order creation, delivery or service execution, invoice matching, payment, reconciliation, and supplier relationship analytics.

With workflow automation, role-based processing, AI-assisted checks, and real-time visibility, each team can act from the same procurement context.

Key features

Procurement control from request to payment.

IProcure brings the core procurement activities into connected workflows, supported by AI, exception handling, and management visibility.

Purchase Request and approval management.

Create requests, validate budgets, and route approvals through configurable matrices and hierarchies.

Vendor sourcing and RFQ comparison.

Manage vendor selection with clearer quotation comparison, evaluation records, and sourcing decisions.

Purchase Order management.

Convert approved requests into controlled purchase orders with traceable ownership, status, and supporting context.

Delivery and service milestone tracking.

Track goods receipts, partial deliveries, pending quantities, and service progress against the procurement plan.

Invoice matching and duplicate detection.

Verify invoices against purchase orders, receipts, services, and payment records to reduce mismatch and duplicate risks.

Vendor performance and analytics.

Monitor compliance, performance, spend, savings, outstanding payments, and procurement KPIs through dashboard-ready data.
