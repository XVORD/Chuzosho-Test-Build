What is ISLD?

A digital, AI-based solution for processing electrical Single Line Diagrams — the 2D representations that describe a panel or circuit board.

INPUT / SLD
OUTPUT / STRUCTURED DATA

The signal inside the drawing

MCBs, fuses, CTs, digital power meters and every associated rating become information you can use.

Simplified workflow

Main purpose

Make diagrams operational.

ISLD extracts useful information from electrical drawings and turns it into structured data. The resulting Bill of Materials gives teams a reliable view of the components that need to be supplied.

From data to decision

A clearer route to a customer quotation.

The Bill of Materials supports estimating the cost of an electrical panel or building project, while clearly specifying what will be provided.

BOM / 01

Components, captured.

Organise detected items with their names, ratings and relevant details.

QUOTE / 02

Costs, estimated.

Use the material list as the basis for preparing a clear customer quotation.

SCOPE / 03

Supply, specified.

Show the components included for an electrical panel or wider building project.

Development loop

Teach the model what matters.

Today, ISLD development includes annotating electrical components in diagrams. These examples teach the machine-learning model the patterns it needs to recognise components automatically.
