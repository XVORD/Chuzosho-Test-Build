The foundry approach

A disciplined route from market requirement to reusable product, with evidence at every important gate.

NEED → CORE → EVIDENCE → INDEPENDENCE

One product system

Shape. Forge. Prove. Graduate.

The sequence is simple on purpose. Each stage answers a different question before the product earns more investment.

SHAPE

Should this exist?

Define the client requirement, user, constraint, and accountable business result.

FORGE

What should repeat?

Build the reusable core and the tools that make it useful across engagements.

PROVE

Can we trust the loop?

Test actions, observations, evidence, cost, and operating behaviour under controlled conditions.

GRADUATE

Can it stand alone?

Prepare the product, channel, governance, and ownership needed for independent growth.

Product discipline

The model is one part of the machine.

Tools, checks, evidence, and observations turn raw capability into a dependable product experience. The surrounding system is where product discipline lives.

Evidence loop

Every turn should make the next turn better.

Actions become observations. Observations become evidence. Evidence improves selection, repair, and continued product learning.

PROPOSE

Generate options, not unchecked answers.

Keep multiple possibilities visible long enough to compare them.

CHECK

Make quality executable.

Translate safety, business expectations, and operating standards into practical gates.

LEARN

Grow an archive, not one winner.

Preserve trajectories and evidence so progress survives a single run.
