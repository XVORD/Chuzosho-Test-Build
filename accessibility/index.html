Accessibility

CHUZOSHO is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

What we are doing

We use semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive alternative text, sufficient colour contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.

Compatibility

The website is designed for current versions of major browsers and common assistive technologies. Some experiences may vary with older software or highly customised settings.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is reviewed as pages and product content evolve. We test layouts at multiple screen sizes and address issues that could prevent people from finding information or completing a task.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need information in another format, email privacy@hoshodigital.com. Please include the page, the issue, and the browser or assistive technology used where possible.
