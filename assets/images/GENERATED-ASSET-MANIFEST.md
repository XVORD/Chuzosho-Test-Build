# Chuzosho generated image manifest

All rendered website imagery in this set is original. The assets below were generated specifically for Chuzosho and replace visual crops previously taken from the reference presentation.

## Shared visual direction

Premium B2B editorial imagery with Japanese-industrial precision, archival patent engraving, and cinematic photoreal industrial design. Palette: warm ivory, black graphite, cast iron, dark steel, aged brass, deep foundry red, and a small oxidized teal accent. Images avoid people, fantasy weapons, Japanese art motifs, cyberpunk city scenes, readable text, logos, and watermarks.
