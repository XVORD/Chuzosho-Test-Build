What cookies are

Cookies are small data files placed on a device when a website is visited. They can help a website operate, remember preferences, understand usage, and improve performance.

How we may use cookies

Necessary cookies

These support core website operation and security.

Functional cookies

These remember choices and enable optional website features.

Analytics and performance cookies

These help us understand how pages are used and where the experience can be improved. Information may be aggregated or pseudonymised where appropriate.

Third-party technologies

Some website functions or analytics may be supplied by third parties. Their technologies are governed by their own privacy and cookie practices.

Managing cookies

You can accept, block, or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may limit parts of the website. Browser help documentation explains the controls available for your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the technologies used by the website change. The current version will be published on this page.
